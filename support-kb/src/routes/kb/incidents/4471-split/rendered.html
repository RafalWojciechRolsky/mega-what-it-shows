The night

Lessons

  • Subject matching merged fast and merged wrong. Two of the three issues in this ticket had no ticket of their own when the desk declared "one issue, one fix".
  • Merge on place and server code (fingerprint), not on the subject a tired reporter typed at 4 a.m. — see decision 0001 for the rule that caused this.
  • The export is the ground truth; a page is a claim. The table and the chart above are computed from the data at render — if the export changes, this page shows it.
